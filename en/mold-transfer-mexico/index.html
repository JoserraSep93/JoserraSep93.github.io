Questions from buyers who already own a mold

Who owns the mold?

You do. The mold is always the customer's property, and it is documented that way from the start.

What if the mold needs maintenance?

We review it on arrival and tell you what it needs. While it produces, our team cleans, inspects and maintains it preventively in the plant.

Is there a minimum volume?

We do not set a general minimum. We evaluate each project by part, mold and delivery frequency; we work best with continuous programs.

Can you ship to the United States?

Yes. We ship from Celaya, Guanajuato, to Mexico and the United States, with packaging defined for each part.
