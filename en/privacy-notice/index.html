Data controller

Data we collect

  • What you provide in the contact and quote forms: name, company, email, phone, city and project information.
  • Files or drawings you choose to send us for a quote.
  • Technical browsing data logged by the server, such as IP address, browser and operating system.

The "Upload your part" viewer processes the STL file in your browser; the file is not sent to our servers unless you attach it to a request.

How we use it

Required purposes: to handle your request, prepare the quote and technical proposal, and follow up on the project.

Additional purposes: to send you information about our services. You can opt out from the first email or at any time; opting out does not affect your request.

Confidentiality of your project

Drawings, models and technical data you share are used only for your project. We sign a non-disclosure agreement before receiving them when you request it.

Transfers

We do not sell or rent your data. We share it only with Grupo Sephnos companies involved in your project, with service providers bound by confidentiality, or when required by law.

Third-party services

The plant video loads from YouTube only when you click play. The WhatsApp button opens that app. Each service has its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You can access, correct, delete or object to the use of your data, or withdraw consent, by writing to marketing@sephnos.com with your name, the right you want to exercise and a contact method.
