What we do in-house and what we coordinate

StageHow it is done
Design reviewIn-house. The group's own Research and Development department: geometry review for manufacturing, resin selection and technical proposal.
MoldCoordinated. Fabrication with specialized tool shops in Mexico and Asia; SDH manages the project, validates samples with you and receives the mold. The mold is always the customer's property.
Existing moldsIn-house. We receive customer molds, check them and mount them with their process sheet.
Mold maintenanceIn-house. Cleaning, inspection and preventive maintenance of molds in production, by specialized staff.
Injection and blow moldingIn-house. Parameters defined per project and recorded in the process sheet.
InspectionIn-house, with acceptance criteria defined with the customer. Critical tolerances with an external metrology laboratory; we do not have our own CMM.
Secondary operationsIn-house, per project: assembly, overmolding, metal component insertion and label application.
PackagingIn-house. Packaging standard defined per project: bulk, boxed or retail-ready.
Storage and shippingStorage in the plant and shipping to Mexico, the United States, Central America, South America or the agreed country, by road, sea or air, under the agreed Incoterm.

Plant and systems

  • Celaya, Guanajuato, in Mexico's Bajío region, with highway access to the northern border and the Pacific ports.
  • Two overhead cranes, 10 and 2 tons, for mounting large molds.
  • 1,500 m² plant and 2,500 m² warehouse for resin and finished goods storage.
  • Roof with solar panels covering part of the plant's electricity consumption.
  • ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015. SAP ERP management system with traceability by batch, machine and shift.
  • Operation Monday through Saturday, with shifts according to project demand.
