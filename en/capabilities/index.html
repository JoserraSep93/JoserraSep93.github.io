Materials

  • Polypropylene (PP)

    General-purpose parts, containers, living hinges, contact with cleaning chemicals.

  • High-density polyethylene (HDPE)

    Buckets, containers, parts for the field and outdoors.

  • ABS

    Housings, parts with good surface finish and rigidity.

  • Polycarbonate (PC)

    Transparent or high impact-resistance parts.

  • Glass-fiber compounds

    Structural parts that need rigidity and dimensional stability.

We also work with the group's own recycled resin when the project allows it and the customer approves it.

What we do in-house and what we coordinate

  1. 01

    Design review

    In-house

    The group's own Research and Development department: geometry review for manufacturing, resin selection and technical proposal.

  2. 02

    Mold

    Coordinated

    Fabrication with specialized tool shops in Mexico and Asia; SDH manages the project, validates samples with you and receives the mold. The mold is always the customer's property.

  3. 03

    Existing molds

    In-house

    We receive customer molds, check them and mount them with their process sheet.

  4. 04

    Mold maintenance

    In-house

    Cleaning, inspection and preventive maintenance of molds in production, by specialized staff.

  5. 05

    Injection and blow molding

    In-house

    Parameters defined per project and recorded in the process sheet.

  6. 06

    Inspection

    In-house

    With acceptance criteria defined with the customer. Critical tolerances with an external metrology laboratory; we do not have our own CMM.

  7. 07

    Secondary operations

    In-house

    Per project: assembly, overmolding, metal component insertion and label application.

  8. 08

    Packaging

    In-house

    Packaging standard defined per project: bulk, boxed or retail-ready.

  9. 09

    Storage and shipping

    In-house

    Storage in the plant and shipping to Mexico, the United States, Central America, South America or the agreed country, by road, sea or air, under the agreed Incoterm.

Plant and systems

  • Celaya, Guanajuato, in Mexico's Bajío region, with highway access to the northern border and the Pacific ports.
  • Two overhead cranes, 10 and 2 tons, for mounting large molds.
  • 1,500 m² plant and 2,500 m² warehouse for resin and finished goods storage.
  • Roof with solar panels covering part of the plant's electricity consumption.
  • ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015. SAP ERP management system with traceability by batch, machine and shift.
  • Operation Monday through Saturday, with shifts according to project demand.
