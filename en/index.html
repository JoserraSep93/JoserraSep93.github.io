From pellet to part

Our plant in Celaya, step by step.

  1. Resin

    PP, HDPE, ABS, PC and glass-fiber compounds. The right resin is defined with you, part by part.

  2. Mold and injection

    125 to 1,300 tons of clamping force. Temperature and pressure documented in the project's process sheet.

  3. Part

    The robot removes the part. Then inspection, packaging to the agreed standard and delivery in Mexico or the United States.

From design to delivery

One team takes your part through all eight stages of the project.

  • Design
  • Validate
  • Coordinate the mold
  • Manufacture
  • Inspect
  • Assemble
  • Pack
  • Ship

  1. Definition

    We review the part's use, volume and requirements.

    Deliverable: Scope and technical proposal

  2. Design

    We adjust the geometry for manufacturing.

    Deliverable: Design for manufacturing review

  3. Mold

    We coordinate its fabrication and validate samples with you.

    Deliverable: Mold, owned by the customer, and approved samples

  4. Production

    We manufacture with parameters defined for the project.

    Deliverable: Process sheet

  5. Inspection

    We check the agreed characteristics before release.

    Deliverable: Inspection report

  6. Secondary operations

    We integrate assembly or other operations when the project requires it.

    Deliverable: Finished part

  7. Packaging and release

    We prepare the product to the agreed standard.

    Deliverable: Packaging and release standard

  8. Storage and shipping

    We coordinate storage and delivery to the agreed destination.

    Deliverable: Delivery in Mexico or the United States

The SDH method

Every part is unique. So is every customer. Every project needs its own route.

Unique part
A manufacturing route specific to each project, documented in its process sheet.
Quality by project
Acceptance criteria defined with the customer before the first run.
Packaging to spec
A documented packaging standard per project. Bulk, blister or retail, as the customer needs it.
Multi-destination deliveries
Mexico and the United States. Logistics is part of the service, not a complication.
Continuous communication
The SDH project leader is your direct contact. Together on every decision, no intermediaries.
Full mold management
We coordinate mold fabrication with a network of specialized tool shops. The mold is always the customer's property.

We understand what your project needs and we are equipped, technically, operationally and logistically, to deliver it successfully, together.
