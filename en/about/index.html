About us

Injection and blow molding contract manufacturing in Celaya, Guanajuato. Part of Grupo Sephnos.

Our story

SDH Plastics is the specialist brand in injection and blow molding contract manufacturing. We operate from Celaya, Guanajuato, the industrial heart of Mexico's Bajío region, serving customers in Mexico and the United States.

SDH Plastics was created to serve industrial companies that need a technical contract manufacturer able to handle the whole project, from design to delivery.

What sets us apart is not what we make. It is how we make it: with consistency, with a technical proposal in every quote, with the capacity to manage the whole chain, from the mold to the first production part, so our customers work with a single partner.

How we care for the environment

Practices already in place at the Celaya plant that support the ISO 14001 certification:

  • Solar panels on the plant roof for photovoltaic generation.
  • Closed-loop water cooling for the machines.
  • Used oils stored and handed over to certified companies.
  • No aggressive materials in the process.
  • Energy-saving lamps and light sensors in the facilities.
  • Recycling of paper, packaging and bags; support for e-waste campaigns.
  • The group's own recycled resin when the project allows it.

Who operates

SDH Plastics is Grupo Sephnos's specialist brand for industrial contract manufacturing. We operate under Sephnos S.A. de C.V., a company with 22+ years of experience in plastics manufacturing, ISO 9001 and ISO 14001 certified, exporting to 50+ countries.

SDH is the brand under which the group serves industrial contract-manufacturing customers. Sephnos S.A. de C.V. is the entity that signs, invoices and operates; your day-to-day contact is the SDH team.
