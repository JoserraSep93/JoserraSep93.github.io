PPAP and first article inspection: the documents industrial customers expect

The 18 PPAP elements, submission levels and what a first article package includes outside automotive.

8 min read · October 20, 2026 · Reviewed by SDH Plastics engineering

The short answer

PPAP is the 18-element package defined in the AIAG manual (4th edition, 2006) that proves your supplier makes the part to print, with a stable process, at the quoted rate. There are 5 submission levels, and Level 3 applies when the customer does not specify another. Outside automotive, the usual ask is a first article package: a full dimensional report, a material certificate and approved sample parts.

What is PPAP and who requires it?

PPAP stands for Production Part Approval Process. Chrysler, Ford and General Motors developed it through AIAG, the North American automotive industry association. The 4th edition was released in March 2006 and is the one in use today.

The goal is for the supplier to prove two things before series production: that it understood every design requirement, and that its process can meet them consistently in a real production run. Automakers and their tier suppliers require it, and a growing number of industrial buyers in other sectors have adopted the same method.

What are the 18 PPAP elements?

The table translates each element into what it means for an injection-molded part. Not every element applies to every part: the design FMEA, for example, is delivered only by whoever owns the design.

What are the 5 PPAP submission levels?

The level defines which documents go to the customer and which stay on file at the supplier's plant, ready for review. Everything has to be prepared; what changes is what gets submitted.

When do you need to resubmit a PPAP?

A PPAP approves a specific combination of part, mold, material, process and location. If one changes, the customer will usually ask for a new submission:

  • A new part going into production.
  • An engineering change on the drawing.
  • A new mold, a replacement mold or added cavities.
  • A change of resin, grade or material supplier.
  • A plant move. If you plan to transfer a mold to Mexico, budget the PPAP as part of the transfer.

What does a 1.67 capability index mean?

The index depends on the tolerance as much as on the process. A tolerance tighter than the part needs drives Ppk down without making the part work any better. That is why it pays to review your injection molding tolerances before cutting steel.

The same goes for measurement: if the gage varies too much, the study fails even when the process is fine. The rule commonly cited from the AIAG MSA manual is that gage R&R under 10% is acceptable, 10% to 30% depends on the application, and over 30% is not acceptable.

What is a first article inspection (FAI) outside automotive?

A first article inspection is a full verification of the first parts made with the final mold and process. In aerospace it is governed by AS9102, which uses three forms: part number accountability, product accountability (materials and special processes) and characteristic accountability for every feature on the drawing.

In appliances, agricultural equipment, construction or consumer products, customers usually ask for a shorter package than PPAP. Ask which format your customer uses before the run, because it changes how many parts get measured and who signs off.

How do you prepare a first article for a molded part?

Common mistake

Approving with parts from the mold trial. Those parts come off settings still being tuned, sometimes on a different press. The first article has to come from the process you are actually buying. The other frequent mistake is measuring only one cavity of a multi-cavity mold: each cavity is a separate cut of steel and can produce different dimensions.

How much time and cost does a PPAP add?

It depends on the level and the number of dimensions. The biggest drivers are the full dimensional layout, capability studies and gage R&R on each gage.
