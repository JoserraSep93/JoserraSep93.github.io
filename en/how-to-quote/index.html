How to quote

With this data we give you a technical proposal and a price per part. The more complete it arrives, the faster we respond.

What we need

DataFormat or detailIf you do not have it
3D model of the partSTEP, IGES, Parasolid or the native file (SolidWorks, Inventor, Fusion). STL works for a first look.Send us a physical sample or photos with dimensions; we model it with you.
2D drawingWith tolerances, surface finish and critical dimensions.Tell us which dimensions matter for the part's use.
MaterialResin and grade, or use requirements: outdoors, chemical contact, impact, temperature, color.We propose the resin based on the use.
VolumeParts per year and lot size or delivery frequency.An estimated range is enough to quote.
MoldIf it exists: type, number of cavities, condition and where it is. If new: desired cavities or target output.We quote the new mold with our tool shop network.
Secondary operationsAssembly, metal inserts, labels, overmolding or others.We define it together in the technical review.
Packaging and destinationHow you want to receive the part (bulk, box, retail) and where: city and country, Incoterm if you have one.We propose the packaging standard and the route.
Target dateWhen you need the first parts.We give you the schedule from mold to first delivery.

What you receive

  1. First technical conversation. We review use, volume and requirements. We tell you whether the part fits our machines and materials.
  2. Design for manufacturing review. Observations on geometry, wall thickness, draft angles and gate locations, when they apply.
  3. Quote. Price per part with material, process, packaging and delivery itemized, plus the mold quote if it is new.
  4. Schedule. Dates for mold, samples, validation and first production.

The information you share is used only to quote. We sign a non-disclosure agreement when you require it.
