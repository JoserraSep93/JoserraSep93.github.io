Case study · Home appliances sector

Appliance manufacturer well known across Latin America

Home appliance program with documentation discipline

What they needed

A large-format plastic injection molding supplier with the quality discipline and delivery consistency a home appliance program demands, where a supplier's monthly variability breaks an industrial OEM's planning. They had their own molds and were looking for a contract manufacturer able to run them to program standards.

Why SDH

Unlike most of our customers, they arrived with their molds already built, so the project went straight to process validation and production. Our large machines (650 to 1,300 tons) cover the structural part needs of home appliances. The operation runs with an ISO system and SAP traceability.

Result

The project is in commercial closing. The relationship was built on the promise of consistency that a home appliance program needs to sustain its manufacturing.

"To run your large-format molds under ISO and SAP documentation discipline: the backing a home appliance program demands."
